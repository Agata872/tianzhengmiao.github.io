A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity meets the physical world.

I explore how machine learning can make distributed wireless networks work better in the real world. My research connects physical-layer signal processing, cell-free massive MIMO, and experiments on real hardware.

I’m a Ph.D. researcher at KU Leuven, supervised by Prof. François Rottenberg ↗. My work is part of EMPOWER-6G ↗.
